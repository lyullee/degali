# Supplementary Information

## S1. Frozen model configuration

The primary validation path uses the corrected expanded flashing source, the
reported E3.5 release-window mean source rate, free ground detachment, a
0.001 m roughness and evaluation at every physical receptor followed by the
same arc-maximum operator used for the observation. The FFI/DNV screen uses
the published low-mast wind and records the 10 m reference-height assumption.
No validation multiplier is fitted.

The detailed configuration and scope boundary are frozen in
[`docs/lh2-paper-baseline-2026-09-20.md`](docs/lh2-paper-baseline-2026-09-20.md).

clock-aligned subset is limited by the transferred raw files (Trials 10–12 and

## S3. Headline metrics

| Lane | MG | VG | FAC2 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| E3.5 peak | 1.047 | 1.425 | 0.839 | frozen primary operator |
| E3.5 synchronised 20 s mean | 0.544 | 4.601 | 0.761 | temporal-operator sensitivity |
| FFI/DNV six-arc screen | 1.245 | 1.373 | 0.833 | independent far-field screen |

Here MG is observed/predicted, so values above one indicate model
underprediction. For retained positive pairs, `r_i = O_i/P_i`,
`MG = exp(mean(ln r_i))`, `VG = exp(mean((ln r_i)^2))`, and FAC2 is the
fraction with `0.5 <= P_i/O_i <= 2`. Non-finite and non-positive pairs are excluded
before the logarithmic statistics. The Hanna limits are descriptive screening
conventions rather than certification criteria. The E3.5 bootstrap interval
and the trial-level residual table are generated by
`tools/audit_lh2_paper_baseline.py` and
`tools/audit_time_aligned_uncertainty.py`.

For the separate paired-path reproducibility run, complete trials were the
resampling unit (5,000 fixed-seed draws). The corrected E3.5 cluster-percentile
intervals are MG 0.771–1.416, VG 1.132–1.882 and FAC2 0.677–0.983. The
corrected FFI intervals are MG 1.031–1.503, VG 1.075–1.754 and FAC2
0.667–1.000 from only two tests. These intervals exclude source,
meteorological and model-form uncertainty; the FFI interval is consequently
fragile and the E3.5 interval is a sensitivity diagnostic, not a confidence
bound on the true atmospheric source.

## S4. FFI Test 6 limitation

At 30 m, the corrected path predicts 6.03/6.72/7.36 vol % for the low/mean/high
reported mast winds against a 21.0 vol % reported arc maximum. The resulting
2.85–3.48 mismatch is retained. The public record does not identify an
independent, synchronised atmospheric two-phase source state; therefore no
source-rate or transport multiplier is fitted to close this residual.

The FFI report also describes two stacked containers, a plastic drum and an
instrument box on the test pad. These structures are not resolved by the
free-ground DEGALI calculation, so the six-arc result is a conditional
far-field/transport screen rather than obstacle validation. The report source,
local hash record and precise boundary are documented in
[`docs/ijhe-ffi-site-geometry-boundary-2026-10-09.md`](docs/ijhe-ffi-site-geometry-boundary-2026-10-09.md).

### S5.1 Bounded dynamic enclosure model

The enclosure-average branch is separate from the 3-D free-field research
solver. For H2 volume fraction `alpha` in effective volume `Veff`, it solves

`d alpha/dt = [Qs(1-alpha) - (Qv+Qb)alpha]/Veff`,

where `Qs` is measured H2 source volume flow, `Qv` is measured mechanical
ventilation and
`Qb = Aeff sqrt[g H alpha(rho_a-rho_h)/rho_a]` is an effective buoyant-opening
exchange. The analytic step includes source displacement, enforces
`0 <= alpha <= 1`, and records injected, removed and stored H2 mass.

Experiment 19 alone identifies mixed-volume fraction, effective opening area
and transport delay. Delay is searched exhaustively from 0 to 30 s at the
nominal 0.3 s sensor interval; the remaining coefficients are bounded to
`0.1 <= Veff/V <= 1.0` and `0 <= Aeff <= 2 m2`. The resulting fixed values are
0.547503, 0.586732 m2 and 5.1 s. Experiments 20, 21 and 23 are then evaluated
without parameter changes.

| Experiment | Role | MAE (vol%p) | RMSE (vol%p) | Pearson r | Observed/predicted peak (vol%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Exp19 | calibration | 0.197 | 0.240 | 0.9920 | 6.992 / 6.504 |
| Exp20 | parameter holdout | 0.265 | 0.314 | 0.9888 | 7.235 / 6.828 |
| Exp21 | parameter holdout | 0.302 | 0.349 | 0.9823 | 5.331 / 4.450 |
| Exp23 | parameter holdout | 0.280 | 0.334 | 0.9742 | 4.318 / 4.289 |

Across the three holdouts, pooled MAE is 0.282 vol%p, RMSE is 0.332 vol%p and
`r = 0.9795`; MAE is 95.62% lower than the same-window ideal well-mixed
baseline. The maximum mass residual is `6.44e-15 kg`. The target is the 900 s
mean of non-flatlined D139--D162 sensors, not the any-sensor envelope.
The three holdouts give spatial-mean 4 vol% first-arrival MAE 3.70 s
(maximum 5.70 s) and flammable-duration MAE 27.1 s (maximum 45.6 s). These
threshold scores use the modeled spatial mean and must not be confused with
the publication's any-sensor alarm operator.
The bound-span-scaled fixed-delay Jacobian condition number is 4.87. A
descriptive 1% SSE profile retains only the 4.8--5.1 s delay grid points, with
mixed-volume fraction 0.5475--0.5529 and effective opening area
0.5852--0.5867 m2; this is an identifiability diagnostic, not a confidence
interval.

The model form was developed with knowledge of the complete four-case public
record. Consequently, this is retrospective parameter-holdout evidence, not
prospective blind validation. The effective opening area is a fitted closure,
not the geometric door area, and the result is not transferable to a new room
without new evidence.

```powershell
.venv\\Scripts\\python.exe tools\\validate_usn_enclosure_dynamic.py
```

## S6. Evidence and reproducibility boundary

Each field-validation package is expected to carry a
`FieldEvidenceManifest` with event/source/weather/obstacle/receptor/common-
clock identifiers, one SHA-256 per selected channel, sensor-set and operator
metadata, and an explicit temporal operator. Missing channel evidence is
`partial`/`withheld`; missing sensor registry or accountable operator metadata
is `conditional`. `promotion_allowed` is always false for a manifest alone.

The public snapshot excludes third-party raw workbooks, report PDFs, original
Fortran and local derived output directories. The source, audit scripts and
derived tables are versioned in the repository; the versioned software record
is identified in `CITATION.cff`.

## S7. Scope exclusions

The reported scores do not establish universal performance for transient puff
clouds, impinging/downward releases, indoor ventilation outlets, complex
obstacle wakes, validated N2/O2 mixed-phase transport or regulatory separation
distances. Those cases remain conditional until a matched source/weather/
geometry/receptor/sensor package is available.

## S8. Public open-channel H₂ timing boundary

The USN/FFI open-channel dataset (DOI `10.23642/usn.26117989.v2`) was audited
as a separate source/sensor timing lane. Twenty-two extracted files parsed
successfully, retaining 638 sensor channels; 455 channels crossed a declared
4 vol% threshold. First-arrival times ranged from 36.89 s to 147.49 s across
the archived runs. Source and sensor clocks remain separate, and raw negative
flow samples are reported rather than clipped. Because weather, site
coordinates and obstacle/common-clock evidence are incomplete, this lane is
`boundary_only` and is not pooled into the atmospheric LH₂ scores.

An additional source-paper crosswalk identifies the article's obstacle cases
O1/O2 as `test-02-1a/b` and `test-05-1a/b`; the dataset README maps these to
`T00027`–`T00030`. The corresponding unobstructed A/E controls are
`test-02-0a/b` (`T00012`/`T00013`) and `test-05-0a/b`
(`T00003`/`T00004`). The paper reports 36.8% blockage and companion LES
cases at 0.091 and 0.45 g/s. These paired trials are now prioritized for
obstacle-effect comparison. All 29 sensor coordinates from the article's
Table 1 are now machine-registered in
`reference/hydrogen/open_channel_usn/obstacle_benchmark_registry_v1.json`.
However, the reported obstacle dimensions do not reproduce the article's
36.8% blockage ratio as a simple rectangular projection, and its position
datum remains ambiguous; the physical solid is therefore withheld. DEGALI's
current 3-D solver also requires a positive streamwise inlet and cannot yet
represent this closed-rear, zero-ambient-flow channel source boundary. These
trials are not yet a DEGALI validation score and do not validate an outdoor
LH₂ release.

Reproduction uses `tools/audit_open_channel_h2_dataset.py`. The resulting
schema is `degali.open-channel-h2-audit.v1` with
`promotion_allowed=false`; the full interpretation is in
[`docs/open-channel-h2-boundary.md`](docs/open-channel-h2-boundary.md).

## S9. Sensor and observation-operator uncertainty boundary

The available transfer package does not contain a calibration certificate or
an instrument-specific probability distribution for every concentration
channel. It therefore does not support an invented “average sensor error” or a
percentage correction applied to the model. The uncertainty components are
kept as separate evidence fields:

The public PRESLHY WP3 rainout dissemination record and its D3.6 report provide
nominal instrument specifications: the Xensor XEN-5320 is listed as 0--100 vol% H₂ with
±3% of full scale, the Dräger Xam 5000 XXSH2 HC as 0--4 vol% with ±2% of
reading, and the Gill Windsonic as ±3% of reading with ±2° direction accuracy;
the cryogenic Coriolis flow accuracy is estimated as ±3% of reading. The same
D3.6 report gives the source-relative receptor coordinates/heights and records
the two obstruction-test offsets (0.16 m and 0.18 m from the release), but not
a channel-level calibration certificate or a surveyed three-dimensional
obstruction mesh.
These are manufacturer/declared specifications, not a channel-level
calibration certificate for the event. In particular, ±3% of a 100 vol% full
scale is an absolute ±3 vol% specification, not a universal ±3% relative error;
near a 4 vol% threshold it can be a large relative contribution. They are
therefore retained as metadata and sensitivity bounds only, not converted into
a fitted residual correction or pooled sensor-accuracy percentage. Source:
the [PRESLHY WP3 rainout dissemination record](https://hysafe.info/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/05/3_4_Coldrick_Rainout_HSE_v2.pdf).

For scale, the nominal specification-only error widths at two decision-relevant
concentrations are shown below. These are arithmetic conversions of the stated
instrument specifications, not measured event errors, confidence intervals or
limits for the model residual.

| Nominal H₂ reading | Xensor XEN-5320 (±3% full scale) | Dräger XXS H₂ HC (±2% of reading) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 vol% | ±3.00 vol% absolute | ±0.04 vol% |
| 4 vol% | ±3.00 vol% absolute | ±0.08 vol% |

The Xensor column illustrates why a full-scale specification cannot be
reported as a 2% or 4% relative sensor accuracy: at those concentrations its
nominal absolute bound is wider than the reading itself. The Dräger values are
smaller nominal bounds, but drift, response time, cross-sensitivity, sampling
line transport and event calibration remain separate unresolved components.
For a nominal 4 vol% indication, the specification-only intervals would be
approximately 1–7 vol% for Xensor and 3.92–4.08 vol% for Dräger. Thus a binary
4 vol% decision is formally indeterminate for the Xensor channel without an
event calibration or an independent sensor; this is a measurement-boundary
statement, not a correction applied to the 11/18 model-operator changes.

| Component | Available evidence | Treatment in this paper |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration bias/precision | No complete channel-level certificate in the transferred package | Not assigned a probability; no residual correction is fitted |
| Sampling-line delay | Approximately 18 s for the E3.5 Xensor alignment | Primary 20 s result uses the declared shift; zero-delay is a separate sensitivity |
| Display/reporting precision | Reported concentration precision is retained in source records where available | Not converted into a confidence interval |
| Sensor position and height | Receptor coordinates and sampled heights are used where available | Model is evaluated at physical receptors before aggregation |
| Temporal observation operator | Peak/arc maximum versus synchronised 20 s mean | Reported as separate validation lanes, never pooled |
| Source/weather/model form | No joint probability distribution in the public package | Deterministic envelopes and conditional labels only |

Consequently, the MAE/RMSE, MG/VG/FAC2 and threshold classifications quantify
the declared model–observation comparison, not pure sensor accuracy. A future
operational qualification package must attach channel-level calibration,
clock-synchronisation and uncertainty metadata to the same
`FieldEvidenceManifest` as the release and weather records.

## S10. Non-LH2 SMEDIS/REDIPHEM transfer boundary

The indexed local evidence inventory contains 28 SMEDIS workbook files and
reduced condition/receptor tables for generic dense-gas experiments. The candidate
records include ammonia, propane, R-12, SO2 and LNG/SF6/CO2 cases, as well as
an EEC control/fence pair with matched reduced sensor locations. They are
useful for a future generic transport or obstacle-observation benchmark, but
they are not LH2 evidence. Their source thermodynamics, static observation
operators and missing channel-level common-clock/calibration package prevent
promotion into the E3.5 or FFI/DNV scores. The obstacle records also do not
identify a complete three-dimensional LH2 wake field.

The data are therefore retained as a separate non-LH2 transfer boundary, not
as additional independent experiments. Original workbooks are third-party
material and are not redistributed. The inventory and promotion decision are
recorded in
[`docs/ijhe-smedis-transfer-boundary-2026-10-09.md`](docs/ijhe-smedis-transfer-boundary-2026-10-09.md).

### S10.1 Additional public sensor-operator boundary records

The public National Laboratory of the Rockies H2SAFE record (DOI
`10.7799/17118570`) contains five indoor helium-release experiments with
time-resolved concentrations, 3-D sensor/source coordinates and laboratory
geometry. It is relevant for testing sensor placement, observation operators
and `FieldEvidenceManifest` ingestion, but helium, indoor ventilation and the
available facility metadata do not constitute LH2 outdoor validation. It is
therefore `boundary_only` and is not pooled with the E3.5 or FFI/DNV scores.

The published NREL HyWAM description supplies a 3-D sampling-frame layout and
instrument context for near-field LH2 monitoring. It does not provide a
complete, independently hashable Trial 10 time-series and calibration package
in the public record used here, so it is retained as geometry/context evidence
only. Neither public record closes the event-level calibration and measured
site-geometry gate for an operational claim.

### S10.2 ELVHYS WP4.2 conditional dynamic and geometry evidence

The public ELVHYS WP4.2 HSE dataset (DOI `10.18710/JXJP0H`) is retained in the
local evidence archive. It contains 48 tests in a nominal 1 m3 transfer
connection space, time-resolved hydrogen concentration, temperature, pressure,
ventilation and weather channels, declared sensor calibration specifications,
and a three-dimensional nozzle/sensor coordinate table. Most channels are
sampled at 20 Hz, with higher-rate pressure-peaking and ignition records.

This lane is retained as `conditional` confined dynamic and obstacle-context
evidence. It is not pooled into the E3.5 or FFI/DNV metrics. The `FLMT` field
is fan/ventilation flow rather than measured hydrogen mass flow, and the walls,
floor, vents and active/passive ventilation define an enclosure problem rather
than the outdoor free-ground model. The pre-registered Test 10/11 screen
therefore reports repeatability and source ambiguity only: the selected
interval medians have PT2 pressures of 1.9297 and 1.7728 barg, nozzle
temperatures of 157.64 and 125.58 K, and Bottom-3 concentrations of 36.73 and
44.53 vol%. No coefficient is fitted and no outdoor score is added.

The archive geometry places the horizontal nozzle at `(20, 500, 250) mm` and
the Bottom-1/Bottom-3 probes at `z = 200 mm`; a final-report table lists a
conflicting nozzle height of 200 mm. The discrepancy is carried forward rather
than hidden. The dataset README also says autumn 2024 while metadata and
filenames give November 2025. The hash-pinned evidence boundary, raw-file
digests and promotion rule are recorded in
[`docs/ijhe-elvhys-conditional-evidence-2026-10-09.md`](docs/ijhe-elvhys-conditional-evidence-2026-10-09.md).

## S12. Public GH2 dynamic, spatial and CFD context evidence

The public USN ventilated car-park record (DOI
`10.23642/USN.14405903`) was ingested from four MATLAB v7.3 files with
publisher MD5 verification. The 24-sensor room-cloud operator reproduces the
published first 4 vol% arrival within 0.498 s and flammable duration within
5.51 s across Experiments 19, 20, 21 and 23. A no-fit 0-D well-mixed mass
balance overpredicts the local observed peak by a mean factor of 1.619 and
substantially overpredicts flammable duration. This is component evidence for
dynamic observation operators, not an outdoor LH2 score.

The separate bounded enclosure response uses Exp19 for coefficient
identification and freezes them for Exp20, Exp21 and Exp23. Its pooled
parameter-holdout MAE/RMSE are 0.282/0.332 vol%p with `r = 0.9795`, versus
6.441/7.422 vol%p and `r = 0.2578` for the same-window ideal well-mixed
baseline. This result is an enclosure-average dynamic score only; the
retrospective model-selection caveat and individual-sensor exclusion remain
explicit.

The HyTunnel-CS Pro-Science record (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.4668554`) was audited
against all seven published MD5 values. The four concentration workbooks yield
15,768 average/maximum/minimum values; the ventilation workbook yields 1,260
signed x-velocity values. The 42 workbook sheets contain 41 H2 labels and one
He label, conflicting with the record's statement of 40 configurations. No
transient common clock, machine-readable measurement height or channel-level
calibration certificate is supplied, and the Inventor CAD has not been
converted into a verified computational geometry. The lane is therefore
`component_evidence_conditional`.

The official MultHyFuel D2.2 report is retained as public CFD context. Its
obstructed ExJet validation case compares CFX, FLACS, OpenFOAM and FRED against
a 12 mm horizontal high-pressure GH2 release into a cylinder array. The report
provides source conditions, sensor coordinates and plotted comparisons but no
machine-readable matched model/receptor table. Because the DEGALI free-field
core does not resolve the array and no identical-operator numerical export is
available, `ranking_allowed=false`. No values are digitised from the plot for
headline statistics. Full hashes, page anchors and gate codes are recorded in
[`docs/ijhe-multifidelity-evidence-2026-10-10.md`](docs/ijhe-multifidelity-evidence-2026-10-10.md).
