# Same-data dose MAPE in the 30-second runtime budget

## Correction of the previous interpretation

The old `mean_absolute_percentage_error=0.8490109647550368` is the **dose**
score on 84 positive observed doses among 145 TI021 sensors. It is not a
54-sensor instantaneous concentration time-series MAPE. TI021 is an R12
dense-gas puff, not hydrogen, and this workbook lacks complete experimental
concentration traces. The dose unit is vol% s. Dividing every observed and
predicted dose by the common 600 s horizon gives average vol%, with identical
MAPE, but does not produce time-resolved experimental concentration accuracy.

## Actual new executions

The new runner is `tools/score_ti021_integral_patch_30s.py`. It uses the same
hash-verified workbook, 600 s event, all 145 sensor coordinates and the same
84-positive-dose scoring mask as the old score. The initial report-scale
holder is a 14 m, twelve-sided, 13 m-high prism at 32 mole% R12, initialized
with exact polygon/cell overlap (2,048.2036 m3; 3,290.1158 kg R12). The local
90 x 140 x 40 m patch encloses the 50 m-radius, 5 m-height fence; its 5 m mesh
has 4,032 cells. The curved fence uses the existing zero-thickness face/link
boundary rather than replacement with a rectangular block. Distant sensors
are sampled through the analytic outlet continuation; no outer grid is marched.

| Run | Runtime including scoring/output | Dose MAPE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Previous nested result | Previously generated baseline | 84.9011% |
| Local patch, 1,000 carrier steps | 4.9342 s | 94.6569% |
| Local patch, 5,000 carrier steps | 22.1207 s | 98.8205% |

The second new run used more carrier steps to examine convergence within the
30 s budget, not fitted source/dispersion coefficients. Both scores were
independently recalculated from the saved NPZ observed/predicted dose arrays.
The 1,000-step positive-sensor predicted dose sum is 1,472.6884 vol% s against
17,317.1294 observed; the 5,000-step sum is 91.0141 vol% s. Thus underprediction
is large even without using small pointwise concentration denominators.

## Interpretation and missing physics

These are failed diagnostics, not validated performance figures. The local
carrier statistical residual is 0.07565 at 1,000 steps and 0.57997 at 5,000,
both above the declared 0.01 tolerance. Longer startup did not establish
convergence. Species mass residuals are small (about 2e-15 relative), but
mass closure does not establish a correct velocity field or sensor exposure.

This fast path omits density-dependent gravity spreading, changes initial
cloud momentum from quiescent to immediately ambient-carried, and freezes the
carrier. These are material departures from the dense-gas experiment. It
retains a neutral background mixing closure and atmospheric wind profile,
but the mean carrier uses a bulk eddy viscosity at the patch's 20 m mid-height,
instead of the old molecular/variable-density momentum path. Downstream
continuation has constant per-receptor diffusivity and lateral/top packet
concentrations are not continued. All these changes are declared in the JSON;
the score is a same-data comparison, not an isolated grid-only ablation.

For this reason, neither the runtime success nor the tiny mass residual is a
usable-accuracy claim. A true instantaneous **H2 concentration MAPE** remains
uncomputed by this TI021 dose test; it requires an H2 time-history dataset.
